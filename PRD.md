# OpsHub product requirements

## Current scope

OpsHub is a self-hosted internal meeting operations workspace. In the present phase, authorised internal users can enter, assign, review, and follow meetings through a responsive browser interface.

## Included in the prototype

- Dashboard with meeting activity and statistics
- Meeting list, meeting detail workflow, and multi-step booking form
- Employee directory and field-agent assignment views
- Attendance/arrival check-in interface with explicit permission states
- Configurable demo alert rule, notification inbox, roles/settings shell, light/dark mode

## Deferred

Customer CRM, customer self-service booking, forms, reporting, backend persistence, authentication, mapping, and real location monitoring are intentionally out of scope for this iteration.

## Location expectations

Any future arrival check-in requests location only after an employee action and consent. Missing readings never prove someone remains at a place. The web product must not represent iPhone background tracking as reliable; that requires a future native app.
